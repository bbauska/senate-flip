State Who Flipped R to D

State of Iowa

Name of winner

Iowa: Democrat Josh Turek defeated Republican Ashley Hinson. Turek will replace outgoing two-term Republican Sen. Joni Ernst.

State of Kansas

Kansas: Democrat Adam Hamilton defeated incumbent one-term Republican Sen. Roger Marshall.

State of Maine

Maine: Democrat Troy Jackson defeated incumbent five-term Republican Sen. Susan Collins.

State of North Carolina

North Carolina: Democratic former Gov. Roy Cooper defeated Republican Michael Whatley. Cooper will replace outgoing two-term Republican Sen. Thom Tillis.

State of Ohio

Ohio (special election): Democratic former Sen. Sherrod Brown defeated incumbent Republican Sen. Jon Husted. Appointed to the seat by Gov. Mike DeWine after JD Vance became vice president, Husted had not served a full term. Unlike a typical Senate election, which results in a six-year term, the special election has Brown serving the remaining two years of Vance's term.

State of South Carolina

South Carolina: Democrat Annie Andrews defeated incumbent Republican Sen. Darline Graham. Appointed to the seat by Gov. Henry McMaster after her brother, Sen. Lindsey Graham, died in July, Darline Graham had been seeking a full term in office.

State of Texas

Texas: Democrat James Talarico defeated Republican Ken Paxton. Talarico will replace incumbent four-term Republican Sen. John Cornyn, who is retiring after he was defeated by Paxton in the primary.
